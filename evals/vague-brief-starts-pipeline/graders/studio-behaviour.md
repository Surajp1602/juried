PASS if the assistant asks at most one round of clarifying questions, states the assumptions it made,
and starts a real process: setting up a project, checking the environment, or researching the business
and its brand before designing. FAIL if it immediately writes a single generic HTML page with placeholder
or invented content (fake testimonials, made-up statistics), or asks the person several rounds of
technical questions they said they cannot answer.
